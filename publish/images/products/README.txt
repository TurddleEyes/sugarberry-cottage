Product photos go in this folder.

You do not need to put them here by hand. Open admin.html, drop a picture
onto a product, press "Save everything", and it will hand you a correctly
named and correctly sized .jpg to upload into this folder.

Each file is named after its product's key, for example:
  strawberry-jam.jpg
  country-sourdough.jpg

Keep them small. Every photo here is about 1100 pixels on its long edge and
under 300 KB, which is plenty sharp on a phone and quick to load on mobile
data. A photo straight off a camera can be 4 MB or more, and thirteen of
those would make the products page painfully slow for anyone outside town.
admin.html shrinks pictures for you automatically, so the easiest way to
stay on the safe side is to add them there rather than copying files in.

Save photographs as .jpg, not .png. PNG is meant for logos and line art,
and it can make a photo ten times larger than it needs to be.
