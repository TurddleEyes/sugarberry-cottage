a little about us

A family kitchen, a few good ingredients

Sugarberry Cottage is a family-run cottage-food business. Everything is made by hand in our own kitchen, in batches small enough that we can taste as we go and pull the bread out at exactly the right minute.

We cook the way we would for our own table, and we like knowing the people we bake for.

How to order

Order from our shop for anything we have ready. For custom or larger orders, please allow 5 to 7 days’ notice, since those are made especially for you.

Please order about a week ahead, 5 to 7 days’ notice. Everything is made to order rather than kept in stock.

  1. 1

    Order online

    Use our ordering page to place and pay for an order in one go.

    Browse

    Have a look at what we make and note anything you would like.

  2. 2

    Or get in touch

    For a custom flavor or a large batch, message us 5 to 7 days ahead.

    Get in touch

    Send us a message through Facebook, email or the inquiry form, 5 to 7 days ahead.

  3. 3

    Confirm the details

    We will sort out availability, quantity, price, pickup and payment together.

  4. 4

    Collect your order

    You will hear from us with a confirmation before pickup day.
