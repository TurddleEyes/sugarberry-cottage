Read this first

I cannot make photographs, and I will not hand you pictures whose licence I cannot check, because using the wrong one can land a small business with a bill. So this page does the next best thing: every button below runs a ready-made search on Pexels or Unsplash, two libraries whose photos are free to use commercially, no credit required, no account needed.

Click a button, pick a picture you like, press their download button, then add it to that product in admin.html.

What makes a listing photo sell

  • Square shape, at least 1280 by 1280. Product cards crop to a square, so a tall photo loses its top and bottom.
  • Show the inside. A cut loaf or a split roll sells better than a whole one. People want to see the crumb.
  • Daylight, no flash. Near a window, mid morning.
  • Keep the backgrounds consistent. Pick photos that share a similar surface and light, so your shelf looks like one kitchen rather than thirteen.

Worth saying plainly: your own phone photos will beat stock every time. Customers can tell the difference, and for a home kitchen the homemade look is the selling point. Use these to open the shop, then swap in your own as you bake.

Photos for the website itself

The pictures on your own pages, the big one on the home page and the one on About Us, are separate from your product photos. Those you drop straight onto the page through the product manager, and it sizes them for you. The same two libraries above work just as well for those.
