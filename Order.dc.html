How to order

Ordering from Sugarberry Cottage is a conversation. Tell us what you would like, we will tell you what is ready, and we will agree on the details before anything gets baked.

Please order about a week ahead, 5 to 7 days’ notice. Everything is baked and cooked to order rather than kept in stock, so that week gives us time to get your order into the schedule.

  1. 1

    Browse the available products

    Have a look through the shelf and note anything you would like. Each product shows its price and whether it is available, seasonal, sold out or coming soon.

    See our products →
  2. 2

    Contact Sugarberry Cottage

    Send us a message on Facebook, by email or phone, or through the inquiry form on the contact page, 5 to 7 days before you need your order. Tell us what you are interested in, how much, and roughly when you would like it.

    Go to the contact page →
  3. 3

    Confirm the details together

    We will write back about what is actually available, the quantity, the price, where and when to collect, and how you would like to pay.

    • Product availability that week
    • Quantity and final price
    • Pickup place and time
    • Payment method
  4. 4

    Receive confirmation before pickup

    Nothing is final until we confirm it. You will get a message from us with the agreed order and pickup details, and then all that is left is collecting it.

Please note

Availability changes

Products, flavors and prices on this website may change without notice. Everything depends on the season, the fruit, and how much we can make in a week. We will always confirm what is available before an order is final.

Payment

Paid at pickup

There is no online checkout on this website. Payment is arranged directly with us and settled at pickup. We will agree the method when we confirm your order. Online payment could be added later if it is ever needed.

Pickup

Where to collect

Pickup is arranged locally, and we will settle the exact place and time with you when we confirm your order. Let us know roughly where you are when you first get in touch.
