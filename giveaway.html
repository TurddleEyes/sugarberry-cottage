The rules

  • No purchase necessary. Entering is free, and buying something does not improve your chances.
  • Illinois residents, 18 and over. Prizes are baked in our home kitchen and handed over in person (If you're Local), or delivered (If you're not local).

    (Disclaimer: We cannot deliver outside Illinois)
  • One entry per person per drawing. Extra comments from the same person are not counted twice.
  • The draw is random and checkable. We collect the comments, draw with a random seed, and publish that seed with the result so anyone can check it.
  • Claim within 48 hours. Winners are named in a comment on the milestone post and have 48 hours to reply or message us. After that we draw again.
  • Local delivery/Shipping. We operate in the Monticello area, so anything farther than about 30 miles from home base we will ship it to you in the mail (For Free!).
  • Tell us about allergies. Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts and tree nuts. See our allergen notice.

Between the drawings we post the in-between hundreds too, so you can see the number moving even when there is nothing to enter.
